## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** I understood that it helps our team decide what to fix first. The Start page gives me a lead issue, shows how it’s trending, and suggests a next step. I could also see where to go to review the other issues.
- **What confused them:** I could follow the navigation, but I couldn’t follow the ranking. Trial activation was labeled “Priority 1 of 5,” and I clicked into the details expecting to learn why it came ahead of cart abandonment. I saw the same 42% figure and recommendation again, so I still didn’t know which issue would have more impact if we fixed it.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** My guess is that it was testing whether leading with one ranked issue, a trend, and a recommended action would help someone like me decide where to focus. It gave me a clear place to start investigating. I’d still need to understand how the issues were ranked before committing the team’s time.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** From the feedback, I need to have the site show validation for the priority ranking
