# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

I walked through the Harbor example: only 2 of 20 seats are active. I chose a team activation plan, assigned Morgan a specific first task, and showed both outcomes—Morgan claims it or doesn’t.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

“The product turns an at-risk customer into a concrete plan for the team. Sharing the plan alone doesn’t count as activation; a teammate has to claim or complete the first task.”

## Where they got confused / pushed back

“If Morgan doesn’t claim the task, what should I do next?”

## What I'll change as a result

I’d change the “Morgan doesn’t claim” branch so the user can recover instead of being sent straight to the readout.

Offer a next step—send a reminder, reassign the task, or take it on themselves—and keep the account marked not activated until the teammate acts. In the readout, show claimed and completed separately; claiming signals engagement, while completing better demonstrates first value.
